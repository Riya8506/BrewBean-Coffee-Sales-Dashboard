# BrewBean Coffee Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

BrewBean Coffee Sales Dashboard is an Excel-based sales and
profitability analysis project built around coffee-shop order data.

The workbook analyzes sales performance, product and category
performance, store and city performance, customer behavior, ordering
patterns, costs, profitability, discounts, payment methods, and customer
ratings through an interactive dashboard.

## Dataset

The main Orders table contains **980 orders** across:

-   6 stores
-   6 cities
-   15 products
-   6 product categories
-   4 seasons
-   3 customer types: New, Returning, Member
-   3 order types: Delivery, Dine-In, Takeaway
-   5 payment modes

The order data also contains date and time fields, store and region
information, customer and loyalty information, product and size
information, quantity, ingredient cost, packaging cost, labour cost,
total cost, selling price, discount, sales, profit, profit margin,
employee, customer rating, and order status.

## Business Questions

-   What is the overall sales and profit performance?
-   Which products and categories generate the most sales and profit?
-   Which stores and cities perform differently?
-   Which periods, months, days, and seasons show different sales
    patterns?
-   How do New, Returning, and Member customers contribute to sales?
-   How do Delivery, Dine-In, and Takeaway orders compare?
-   How do costs, discounts, sales, and profit relate to one another?
-   How do payment modes differ across orders?
-   What customer-rating patterns are visible in the data?

## Data Preparation and Analysis

The workbook's main Orders table contains prepared fields for analysis,
including:

-   Date, month, quarter, season, and day information
-   Store, city, and region fields
-   Customer type and loyalty tier
-   Product, category, and size
-   Quantity and selling price
-   Ingredient, packaging, labour, and total cost
-   Discount, sales, profit, and profit margin
-   Payment mode and order type
-   Customer rating and order status

## Dashboard Analysis

The workbook includes a dedicated **DASHBOARD** sheet and a separate
**BUSINESS INSIGHTS** sheet.

The analysis covers:

-   Sales performance
-   Profitability
-   Product and category performance
-   Store and city performance
-   Customer behavior
-   Time and seasonal patterns
-   Order-type analysis
-   Payment-mode analysis
-   Discount and cost analysis
-   Customer-rating analysis

The workbook also contains interactive slicers for categories, cities,
customer types, months, payment modes, and stores.

## Business Insights

A separate **BUSINESS INSIGHTS** sheet is included to communicate deeper
observations from the dashboard analysis and connect the analysis to
